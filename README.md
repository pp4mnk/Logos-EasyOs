# Logos-EasyOs
.SVG LOGOS EASYOS .- 

**EasyOS Collection — A lot of  Professions and Themes**

Give your EasyOS desktop a personal touch with this collection of  illustrated logo variations, featuring professions, seasonal designs and imaginative characters.

From programmers, teachers, firefighters and police officers to astronauts, musicians, pirates and wizards, each design adds a different personality while keeping the familiar EasyOS face recognisable.

**Credits and original artwork**

Modified and customised by **pp4mnk**, based on the original artwork by **Barry Kauler**. The original facial design has been preserved, with additional accessories, colours and themed elements.

**File format and features**

- All  logos are supplied as **editable SVG vector files**.
- Transparent backgrounds make them easy to place over different colours and images.
- Vector artwork can be resized without losing sharpness.
- Colours, shapes and accessories can be adjusted individually in a vector editor.
- Profession designs are stylised illustrations and do not reproduce official badges or insignia.

**How to use the logos**

Extract the ZIP archive and choose a design. The included preview sheet helps you browse the collection.

You can use these variations for:

- **Desktop customisation:** add a character to your wallpaper or use it as a desktop or launcher icon where custom images are supported.
- **Websites and blogs:** illustrate EasyOS articles, tutorials, announcements or personal project pages.
- **Forum avatars:** choose a character that reflects your interests or profession.
- **Educational resources:** decorate classroom materials, presentations or technology activities.
- **Seasonal designs:** give screenshots, wallpapers or posts a Christmas, Halloween, winter or spring theme.

Use the SVG directly in applications that support it. If an application requires a bitmap image, export a PNG at the size you need.

**Editing with Inkscape**

1. Open your chosen SVG in **Inkscape**.
2. Select the element you want to customise. If necessary, ungroup the artwork to access individual parts.
3. Change colours using the palette or the **Fill and Stroke** panel.
4. Move, resize, remove or modify accessories to create your own variation.
5. Save a separate SVG copy to keep your customised version editable.
6. To create a PNG, open the export panel, choose the page or drawing area, set the desired dimensions and export. Keep the background transparent if needed.

For small icons, favour simpler designs and check that the accessories remain clear at the final display size.

**Unofficial personalisation collection**

These variations are personalisation proposals, **not official EasyOS logos**. They do not imply endorsement by Barry Kauler or the EasyOS project.

When sharing the collection or your own adaptations, please retain the attribution to **Barry Kauler for the original artwork** and **pp4mnk for these modifications**.
